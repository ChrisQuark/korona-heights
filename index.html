THE IDEA BEHIND KORONA HEIGHTS

Space to belong.

Seventeen flat-faced spruce glulam homes, set across three garden terraces in Monagroulli. Grey pitched roofs shelter bright living rooms and a private master suite above. Sage, terracotta and natural oak bring a Scandinavian warmth to the interiors.

01 / Upper

Plots 6 and 5
Three homes on each plot.

02 / Middle

Four homes on plot 4 and two on plot 3,
aligned as one considered row.

03 / Lower

Three homes on plot 1.
Two homes on plot 2.

The upper homes arrive directly from the public road above the plots, leaving their garden fronts for outdoor living. The middle homes share one building line. On the lower terrace, the homes follow plot 1’s angled boundary and step apart within plot 2’s tapering shape. Walls and planting define private gardens while keeping the entrance routes clear.

The green area brings the village together with a small pool, a sheltered café terrace, a tennis court and a garden gym. Every house has a garden, a concrete outdoor kitchen and a parking space.

 FIND YOUR WAY

A world to wander.

Explore

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. Select a home or a plot on the masterplan.

Bird’s-eye

A view straight down onto the site. Drag to pan and scroll to zoom. Switch back to Explore to orbit.

Walk

Use W A S D or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to move faster. Click to capture the mouse or drag to look. Esc releases the mouse. On a touch screen use the joystick and drag on the right to look. Use the living room and master suite buttons to arrive inside.

Measure

Select two points on any visible model surface. Keep selecting pairs to compare distances. Enable vertex snapping for corners. Dragging still moves the camera. Undo removes a point or your last measurement. Export saves all distances and coordinates.

Make it yours

Choose Bedroom, Dining or Library in the residence panel. Switch to Night using the moon button, change the sunlight, lift the roofs, show plot boundaries or adjust the terrace levels in View settings. The camera button saves the current 3D view.
