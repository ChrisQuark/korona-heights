THE DESIGN DECISION

More front doors.
Less complexity.

Olive Courtyards is a fresh commercial concept: 22 two-bedroom homes in six small rows. Each has its own entrance and outdoor room, with a bedroom floor above a sociable kitchen and living space.

Build simply

Regular spans, stacked wet rooms and repeated kitchens. Conventional local construction with warm lime render, stone details and timber shutters. Separate acoustic wall leaves between homes.

Keep life outdoors

Private courtyards with a built-in grill and dining bench. Olive trees, front-door footpaths, a 4 × 10 m residents’ pool on plot 6 and a 32 m² wellness studio on plot 5.

Make the numbers visible

Whole-project economics include the land’s €1.2m opportunity cost. The ownership split is open. Cost targets and selling prices are assumptions to test with local tenders and valuers.

Use the land carefully

Each plot stays within its own assumed 30% allowance. No transfer of building rights is assumed. The public green and utility reservation remain clear of private buildings.

WHAT THIS MODEL DOES — AND WHAT STILL NEEDS CONFIRMING

A measured concept.

Floor-area budget7,666 m² × 30% = 2,299.8 m². Proposed: 2,184 m² in homes + 32 m² studio = 2,216 m². Reserve: 83.8 m².
Plot allocationFour homes each on plots 3, 4, 5 and 6; three each on plots 1 and 2. Each plot remains below its own assumed 30% cap.
Height & coverageTwo floors; homes approximately 6.77 m above their provisional ground level, below the binder’s 8.30 m limit. Building footprint: 1,124 m², before pool and landscape structures. No third floor or roof accommodation.
SetbacksHomes are checked against a 3 m inset of the traced plots. This is a design assumption supported by dimensions in the old binder, not confirmation of the setback rules for the new scheme.
RoadThe existing model’s road route, widths and levels are retained. The 2021 permit specifies 7 m / 5.5 m carriageways for its 11 m / 9.10 m reservations. An engineer must reconcile those segment requirements with the current model; new parking courts do not change the road alignment.
Green & utilitiesThe 2021 subdivision permission requires public green, utilities and two public accessible parking spaces. These rights and reservations take priority. Private leisure buildings are placed on residential plots; no free building allowance in the public green is assumed.
Planning statusThe binder records H6, 20% density, 20% coverage and two floors. The future 30% density is sponsor-reported and unconfirmed. The September 2021 subdivision permission has a five-year validity clause; current validity, implementation or extensions must be checked.
Further design workThe authority must confirm housing type, density, parking, building separations, emergency access, accessibility, drainage, pool operation and energy requirements. Ground investigations and a current survey must establish retaining walls and final levels. This is not a permit or construction set.
