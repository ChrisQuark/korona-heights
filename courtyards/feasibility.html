THE INVESTMENT DECISION

Preferred among the concepts screened.
Not yet ready for an unconditional investment.

The illustrative base case produces €384,240 before tax, a 6.0% sales margin, after including the land at €1.2m. A 10% reduction in selling prices produces a loss. The scheme therefore needs firmer sales evidence, a better construction tender, or revised land economics. More homes alone do not guarantee a better return.

For a 15% sales margin at the base selling prices, the building tender would need to be about per gross m² with the other assumptions unchanged. That is a target to test, not an available local quote. The live calculator below lets you make your own assessment.

Gross includes walls and both stair projections. Clear interior excludes walls and the upper stair opening, counting the stair footprint once. These are model areas, not certified saleable or statutory areas. This differs from the usable-area labels on the two original timber studies.

Who would buy?

Couples, smaller families and people working in eastern Limassol who value outdoor space and a private entrance. The product should also be tested with downsizers, although the bedroom stairs limit accessibility. Two bedrooms and compact gardens place a ceiling on the price.

What earns its keep?

Repeatable kitchens and bathrooms, shared wall lines, regular structural spans and no lifts. Use local contractors for conventional construction, warm lime render, modest stone accents and timber screens. A small pool and 32 m² wellness studio support the offer without a staffed café or expensive sports facilities.

06 / PLANNING GATES

Permission first. Commitment second.

What the drawings support

The July 2022 binder records H6, 20% density, 20% coverage, two floors and an 8.30 m height limit. The new model is two floors and about 6.77 m high. The 2,216 m² proposal leaves 83.8 m² below a future 30% floor-area budget.

Each plot is within its own assumed 30% allowance. Home footprints sit at least 3 m inside traced residential boundaries. Building footprint is 1,124 m² before pool and landscape structures; conservative pool inclusion also fits the assumed 20% per-plot coverage check.

What is not yet established

The 30% change is sponsor-reported and unconfirmed. The 2021 permission covers subdivision into six plots, public green, utilities and two public accessible parking spaces. It does not approve these homes. Its five-year validity clause means implementation or extensions need current confirmation.

The road alignment stays as in the existing model. The permit specifies 7 m and 5.5 m carriageways by road reservation; an engineer must reconcile these with the model’s widths. Parking approval, emergency access, housing standards, separation, accessibility, drainage and final levels remain design tasks.

The public green is not spare private building land. The pool sits on plot 6 and the wellness studio on plot 5. The model proposes 44 private parking bays, including six designated wider bays; these do not replace the two public accessible spaces required by the subdivision permission. Final boundaries, setbacks, title rights and access grades require a current survey.
