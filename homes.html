DESIGNED FOR A DIFFERENT PACE OF LIFE
THE IDEA BEHIND KORONA HEIGHTS

Space to belong.

Sixteen flat-faced spruce glulam homes, set across three garden terraces in Monagroulli. Grey pitched roofs shelter bright living rooms and a private master suite above. Sage, terracotta and natural oak bring a Scandinavian warmth to the interiors.

aligned as one considered row.

03 / Lower

Three homes on plot 1.
Two homes on plot 2.

The upper homes arrive directly from the public road above the plots, leaving their garden fronts for outdoor living. The middle homes share one building line. On the lower terrace, the homes follow plot 1’s angled boundary and step apart within plot 2’s tapering shape. Walls and planting define private gardens while keeping the entrance routes clear.

The green area brings the village together with a small pool, a sheltered café terrace, a padel court and a garden gym. Every house has a garden, a concrete outdoor kitchen and a parking space. Beyond the plots, gentle hills, vineyard rows and fruit orchards create an illustrative countryside setting.

Dimensions & assumptions

The manufacturer’s timber, roof and glazing concept is developed into two measured designs. Each keeps the same 16 house centres and orientations. The selected option’s architectural plans describe its area calculation and proposed site arrangement.

Roads & entrancesPublic road and narrow southern connection follow the corridors in the binder and subdivision drawing. Added internal streets and the eastern return loop have been removed.  FIND YOUR WAY

A world to wander.

House sizes & plans

Choose 95 m² or 143.7 m² in the top bar. Floor plans opens the matching drawing set. Market research opens the investor paper and calculator for the selected size. Info opens your shared folder. Smooth mode lowers foliage detail, resolution and lighting effects; the View settings menu also offers Balanced and High. Measurements and dimensions stay the same at every quality.

Explore

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. Select a home or a plot on the masterplan.

Bird’s-eye

A view straight down onto the site. Drag to pan and scroll to zoom. Switch back to Explore to orbit.

Walk

Move the mouse over the scene to look freely; click for captured mouse look. W A S D walks and the arrow keys turn your head. Press L or the WASD button to use W A S D for looking instead. Hold Shift to walk faster. Esc releases and pauses mouse look; click the scene to resume. On a touch screen use the joystick and drag on the right to look. Use the living room and master suite buttons to arrive inside. The private gates slide open as you approach and close when you leave.

Your garden

Select a home and choose Visit the private garden or Garden from above. Each garden is enclosed by a stone perimeter. Street lamps, garden festoons and lights around the shared spaces turn on at Dusk and Night in View settings.

Measure

Select two points on any visible model surface. Keep selecting pairs to compare distances. Enable vertex snapping for corners. Dragging still moves the camera. Undo removes a point or your last measurement. Export saves all distances and coordinates.

Make it yours

Select a house or open Residences. In its panel choose Bedroom, Dining or Library for the lower-left room, then use the visit button or Ground-floor plan. The separate Master suite selector offers Open suite or Walk-in wardrobe; use Visit the master suite or Loft plan to compare. Each choice saves for that house, with an option to apply it to all 16. Switch to Night using the moon button, change the sunlight, lift the roofs, show plot boundaries or adjust the terrace levels in View settings. The camera button saves the current 3D view.
